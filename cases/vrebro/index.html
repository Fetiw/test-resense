Re.bro Restaurants

Development of a flexible website for a restaurant chain with the ability to scale and add new restaurants worldwide, implement split payments, and ensure the delivery of juicy burgers and ribs.

Open Website

Challenge

The main goal of the project was to develop a flexible and easily scalable website that could function correctly as both an information hub about restaurants in any country worldwide and a platform for ordering favorite dishes online. Additionally, it aimed to convey the juiciness and flavor of signature dishes through the device screen.

Idea

First, we developed a concept for photographing the restaurant's dishes that could be easily replicated anywhere. We validated this hypothesis by shooting and recreating all the dishes from the website's main page while being in Ukraine, even though the dishes were served in Poland.

Pop Ups

The website we developed includes 22 pop-up windows that dynamically adjust their color based on the background of the page you are currently on.

Stop Motion

We implemented the dish presentation style using stop-motion animation, creating a frame-by-frame video imitation in .jpg and .webp formats. This approach optimized the website's loading speed.
